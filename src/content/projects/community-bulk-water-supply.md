Phehlwana Group operates a fleet of Powerstar bulk water tankers supplying potable water to households, standpipes, and worksites across Gauteng. The service covers scheduled community relief during municipal water interruptions, as well as bulk water supply to construction and civil sites that need water for dust suppression, compaction, or general site use.

## Service Scope

- Bulk water collection from approved municipal or borehole sources
- Tankering and on-site delivery to households, community standpipes, and construction sites
- Scheduled relief supply during municipal water interruptions and planned maintenance
- Dust suppression and compaction water supply for active construction and road sites
- Rapid response dispatch for urgent water shortages

## Fleet

Our water tanker fleet delivers directly to site, with drivers and support crew managing safe, hygienic dispensing at each stop. Tankers are available for once-off delivery or scheduled recurring runs.

## Outcome

The programme has provided reliable interim water access to affected communities during supply interruptions, and dependable bulk water supply to active construction sites requiring water for dust suppression and compaction throughout the build programme.
